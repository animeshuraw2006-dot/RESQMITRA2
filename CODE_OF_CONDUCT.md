# 🤝 RESQ-MITRA Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors and project leaders of RESQ-MITRA, pledge to make participation in this community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community. We share a common goal: building technology that helps people seek and coordinate assistance with dignity and respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people the app is intended to help.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.
- Respecting cultural, regional and language differences.
- Handling reports of emergencies, safety concerns and vulnerabilities with care.
- Protecting the privacy of help seekers, responders and reporters.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Sexualised language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal attacks or threats.
- Public or private harassment, intimidation or discrimination.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing emergency locations, contact details, photos, voice recordings, identity documents or incident descriptions without authorisation.
- Using the project to stalk, impersonate, threaten, exploit or intentionally endanger another person.
- Submitting knowingly false emergency reports or abusing responder workflows.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional and safety-focused community.

## Privacy and Emergency-Data Respect

RESQ-MITRA may process phone numbers, emergency contacts, location information, photos, voice notes, medical context and other sensitive information. Contributors and maintainers must:

- Use synthetic or consented data in examples, screenshots, tests and bug reports.
- Never commit API keys, passwords, access tokens, personal phone numbers, identity documents or real emergency media.
- Never publish a real person's location or incident details in a public issue or pull request.
- Obtain appropriate permission before collecting, using or sharing another person's photo, voice or other personal information.
- Use sensitive data only for the purpose and duration authorised by the person or applicable policy.
- Report suspected exposure of sensitive data privately and promptly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all RESQ-MITRA community spaces, including the source repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, project communication channels and events. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Maintainers should explain decisions where practical and apply the rules consistently.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers through the repository's GitHub contact channels. For sensitive matters, contact a maintainer privately rather than publishing personal information or incident evidence in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, to the extent reasonably possible. Community leaders will respect the privacy and security of reporters and will share report details only with people who need them to investigate or respond. The project may remove content, edit or close contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove a participant from project spaces when warranted.
